## STAT-01: observations, independence and pairing

A latency observation is one correctly completed legal workload cycle, measured from its predeclared intended arrival through its final required bytes, effect and protocol completion. Independently paced arrivals preserve queueing/admission delay. Setup starts before connect; rejection starts at offered arrival and ends with a valid refusal. Report client/service/tool clocks, synchronization and observer uncertainty. Full-cycle CPU includes authentication, reconnects, rekeys, all service helpers, attributable OS work and cleanup; user-workload CPU is also disclosed separately. Refused attempts never count as useful throughput. CPU at matched useful work differs from CPU at saturation.

The independent statistical unit is one **paired whole repetition** of candidate and reference, using matching workload/seed/fixture state, equal allowances and no competing measurement. Requests, channels, connections and time-series samples inside it are correlated subsamples; they provide the window's empirical distribution and totals, not extra independent repetitions. Restore the specified logical initial state between repetitions, retaining precisely the history demanded inside each cycle. Use distinct precommitted seeds and predeclared arrival traces. Odd-numbered pairs run reference then candidate and even-numbered pairs candidate then reference, through the six planned pairs and any seventh/eighth pair. Pair matching does not permit simultaneous competing runs. Freeze order before results.

A common reset/source/client or thermal/storage/network carryover that correlates whole pairs invalidates independence. Preserve such observations; do not cure them by relabeling1000 requests as independent. Independent seeds, restored fixtures, released preceding workload/resources, equal predeclared quiet state and separate noninterfering measurements are required operational controls. A known unreleased workload or shared disturbance across pairs is an invalid series. Absence of detected correlation is not proof of independence; retain that limitation. The chosen distribution-free median interval avoids a further unverified normal-log-ratio model or evaluator-selected fitting procedure.

## STAT-03: point, uncertainty and decision

The point is the ordinary sample median of the paired raw ratios: average the third/fourth ordered values at n=6, or fourth/fifth at n=8. Retain ties, including values exactly at the gate. The unguarded uncertainty interval is the closed interval from the smallest to the largest paired ratio. No resampling seed, percentile interpolation, logarithmic model or evaluator library default enters this rule.

For independent identically distributed whole-pair ratios and any population median, the chance that all n observations lie strictly on one side of that median is at most 2divided by 2to the power n. Thus this interval has at least 96.875% coverage at six pairs and 99.21875% at eight; retained ties make the bound conservative. The two possible looks together have at least 96.09375% pointwise simultaneous coverage by the union bound, exceeding the declared minimum 95% even when variability selects the second look. These are per-row/per-metric guarantees under independence/stationarity, not95% simultaneous confidence over the whole matrix or confidence for an individual client's true tail distribution. The p99 comparison estimates the median ratio of whole-window empirical p99 s; it is not a claim that1000correlated requests establish a population-tail interval.

The authoritative basis is the binomial/order-statistic construction in [NIST median confidence limits](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman1/auxillar/mediancl.htm) and the binomial sign principle in [NIST sign test](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman1/auxillar/signtest.htm); this exact noninterpolated min/max choice and two-look bound are derived here rather than attributed as NIST defaults. [NIST autocorrelation guidance](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda331.htm) motivates the independent whole-pair unit. Small-n mean/t alternatives were considered and rejected because no normal log-ratio model is supplied. A zero observed spread still does not establish zero measurement error.

Each positive quantity must have validated absolute resolution and relative calibration uncertainty no worse than 1% of its own measured quantity and also no worse than 1% of the reference quantity; otherwise the ratio is unresolved. Expand the ratio interval downward by multiplying by 0.99/1.01 and upward by 1.01/0.99. This fixed one-percent-per-quantity guard remains even for identical observations. It is a declared conservative measurement allowance, not extra performance tolerance. Compare exact unrounded interval endpoints to exact thresholds.

For higher-is-better throughput/responsive capacity, threshold T is 0.70. Pass requires point at least T **and** guarded lower limit at least T. Fail if guarded upper limit is strictly below T. Otherwise Inconclusive. For lower-is-better CPU/memory/latency/recovery costs, T is 1/0.70, exactly 10/7. Pass requires point no greater than T **and** guarded upper limit no greater than T. Fail if guarded lower limit is strictly greater than T. Otherwise Inconclusive. An endpoint exactly at T passes the corresponding non-strict pass boundary; it does not create strict failure on the other side. A point exactly at T normally remains Inconclusive because of the uncertainty guard. Apply valid absolute/correctness failures first. For each metric,next check its own pairing,sample,resolution,denominator,accounting and independence eligibility;an invalid/unresolved metric is Inconclusive and its numerical interval cannot establish either Pass or Fail. Only for an eligible metric,apply strict final-interval failure,then finalV>0.10as Inconclusive,then the interval pass rule;remaining cases are Inconclusive. Failures override Inconclusive/Pass at the overall mandatory gate; otherwise all required gates must pass. Unresolved actual failures are never erased by later inconclusive attempts.
